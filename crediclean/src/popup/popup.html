How it works

  1. Open a ChatGPT conversation that contains an image.
  2. Look for the small CC button on the image.
  3. Click it to see what provenance data the image contains.
  4. Choose whether to save a copy with the credentials removed.

Privacy

Images are read and processed inside your browser. CrediClean does not upload your images, prompts or conversations anywhere, and has no analytics or accounts. The only network requests it makes are to download the image you clicked on, from OpenAI's servers.

What this cannot do

Removing metadata does not remove invisible watermarks carried in the pixels, and does not make an image undetectable as AI-generated. An image with no credentials is not proof that it was made by a person.
